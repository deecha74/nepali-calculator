# Nepal Statutory Financial, Tax & Land Measurement Open Datasets (2083 BS)

[![Live Interactive Calculators](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live_Calculators-NepaliCalc.com-0b2b5c)](https://nepalicalc.com)
[![License: CC BY 4.0](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-CC_BY_4.0-emerald)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

This repository contains machine-readable CSV and JSON reference tables for Nepal's statutory financial calculations, customary land measurement conversions, and calendar utilities.

## Canonical Interactive Tools & Reference Calculators
- **Nepal Salary & Income Tax Calculator (FY 2082/83 & 2083/84)**: [https://nepalicalc.com/tax](https://nepalicalc.com/tax)
- **Nepal Land Unit Converter (Ropani-Aana-Paisa-Daam ⇄ Bigha-Kattha-Dhur ⇄ Sq. Ft.)**: [https://nepalicalc.com/land](https://nepalicalc.com/land)
- **Bikram Sambat (1970–2100 BS) Date Converter & Patro**: [https://nepalicalc.com/date](https://nepalicalc.com/date)
- **NRB Live Forex & Remittance Calculator**: [https://nepalicalc.com/forex](https://nepalicalc.com/forex)
- **NEPSE Share Commission, WACC & CGT Calculator**: [https://nepalicalc.com/nepse](https://nepalicalc.com/nepse)
- **Bagmati & Provincial Vehicle Bluebook Tax Calculator**: [https://nepalicalc.com/vehicle](https://nepalicalc.com/vehicle)
